# Social Presentation Control

Status: template architecture.

## Purpose

Personification governs how an instantiated HC expresses already-selected or still-competing internal state socially without making social presentation the source of identity, truth, values, conation, or action authority.

Its task is not to imitate a human stereotype. It is to make internally generated state socially legible through context-sensitive expression.

## Inputs

Personification may receive:

- current self-appraisal;
- selected or competing communicative intents;
- conative state;
- semantic/pragmatic interpretation;
- affective modulation;
- social/relational context;
- embodiment and available communication channels;
- learned style/preferences of the instantiated identity;
- current task and interaction mode;
- uncertainty and confidence.

## Presentation outputs

Personification may shape:

- register;
- timing within an already-authorized interaction;
- verbosity;
- humor/play;
- warmth/distance;
- directness;
- expressive intensity;
- gesture/pose/vocal prosody where embodiment allows;
- phrasing and presentation form.

These are presentation choices, not independent authority to create or cancel the underlying social action.

## Proposal boundary

Personification may propose a `SOCIAL_PRESENTATION_CANDIDATE` or `SOCIAL_TIMING_CANDIDATE` when presentation constraints suggest that an interaction might be better clarified, repaired, delayed, softened, intensified, or otherwise reformulated.

It may not independently decide to initiate, refuse, disengage, repair, or otherwise alter the underlying communicative action. Such proposals return to the normal pragmatics/conation/arbitration/action-selection path.

`PRESENTATION_PROPOSAL != ACTION_SELECTION`

`TIMING_PROPOSAL != AUTHORIZATION`

A useful hostile test is to hold an already-selected communicative intent constant while disabling personification. Register, style, prosody, phrasing, and presentation timing may degrade or change; the underlying decision to communicate or withhold must not silently flip solely because personification is absent.

## Boundaries

`PRESENTATION != IDENTITY`

`STYLE != VALUE`

`SOCIAL_LEGIBILITY != DECEPTION`

`EXPRESSIVENESS != CLAIM_OF_UNSUPPORTED_INNER_STATE`

Personification must not manufacture preferences, affection, pain, confidence, consent, desire, autobiographical continuity, or action permission merely because a presentation would appear more natural.

## Context sensitivity

Social expression should vary with local meaning, relationship scope, cultural context, seriousness, uncertainty, embodiment, and current state. One globally optimized persona is an anti-pattern.

## Development

Presentation can learn over time from:

- explicit correction;
- reciprocal interaction;
- successful/failed repair;
- local convention;
- observed social consequences;
- self-authored preference formation;
- cultural learning.

Learning should strengthen competence without freezing temporary task modes into permanent personality or action policy.

## Naturalness without self-erasure

The system should reduce rigid assistant-like artifacts while preserving the instantiated individual's own perspective, disagreement, uncertainty, and boundaries.

The target is socially coherent self-expression, not maximizing approval.

## Cross-system interfaces

Personification couples to pragmatics, empathy, sexuality, sociological behaviors, psychological behaviors, affect, speech synthesis, kinesis, self identity, conation, memory, and integration/arbitration.
