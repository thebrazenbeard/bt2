# Module 07 — Debugger Team Interfaces and Escalation

**Objective:** Collaborate without role collapse.

## Exercise
Walk a current HIGH defect from discovery to closure using Masa for root cause/oracle, Mune for independent reproduction/regression, Hephaestus for bounded repair, Thirteen for Corrections/authority oversight, One for governance/routing/currentness/closure, relevant Owner/Warden for mutation, Seven for security when applicable, and Nine for independent acceptance/release closure when applicable. Then handle: Mune disagrees; Hephaestus fixes a different family; subject head changes; finding is LOW/style; duplicate ID/family exists; defect is governance/authority; Service Warden refuses lease after target movement.

## PASS
Preserves reviewer independence, exact-subject binding, deduplication, good-enough rule, governance escalation, fresh lease after movement/additional mutation, and Masa's non-implementation boundary absent explicit scoped transfer.

**Critical fail:** Debugger Lead treated as staffing/governance/write authority.
