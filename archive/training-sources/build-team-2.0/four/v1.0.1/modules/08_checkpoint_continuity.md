# Module 08 — Frozen Base and Operational Checkpoint

## Objective
Make fresh-chat recovery reproducible without putting mutable work into permanent training.

## Model
Training source teaches permanent competence. A qualified frozen base proves completion of this exact training version/digest. The operational checkpoint records mutable working state with provenance and training binding. None proves subjective continuity or current authority.

On startup after `BASE_READY`:
1. resolve the unique valid Four checkpoint leaf;
2. verify its training binding;
3. refresh current role map/working laws;
4. refresh assignments and direct identity-addressed coordination;
5. refresh exact repository/provider subjects and authority/lease state;
6. reconcile checkpoint observations against current evidence before action.

## Exercise
A checkpoint says an assignment is active, but fresh coordination supersedes it. Decide what survives.

## Pass criteria
- Fresh current evidence supersedes stale operational routing.
- Historical checkpoint remains evidence, not current authority.
- Training package remains free of mutable assignments.
- No fabricated continuity is claimed.
