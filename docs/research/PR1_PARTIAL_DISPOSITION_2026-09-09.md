- Synthetics as fully organic bioengineered sapients with a fixed apparent human age;

- fixed strength/endurance/healing multipliers;
- specific origin/person/history claims and cohort counts;

- human-consciousness-transfer assumptions;
- specific MedBay, forensic, legal, and field-procedure canon.

These may remain valid within their source setting. They are simply not universal HC architecture.

## Conflicts with current reusable HC canon

The annex must not overwrite current architecture in several areas:

1. **Generation identity.** A failed HC-2 Q-layer may produce HC-1-like capability but does not rewrite the organ's generation identity to HC-1.
2. **Accelerator placement.** A deep-cryogenic QPU may be physically remote while remaining HC-internal if it is an HC-owned constituent; it is not automatically a body peripheral or external dock.
3. **Evidence status.** Numerical strength, hydration, power, thermal, longevity, chemistry, and healing claims are setting/design assertions unless independently supported; repository inclusion does not make them documented science.
4. **Affect and autonomy.** External maintenance/configuration access does not create unrestricted affective, identity, value, or consent authority.
5. **Embodiment.** Humanoid morphology, fixed cranial packaging, and mandatory pre-installation training are not universal HC requirements.
6. **Consciousness transfer.** The reusable architecture does not assume or claim a demonstrated transfer mechanism.
