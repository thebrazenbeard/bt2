# HC-3 Noöplex-EQ

Status: canonical generation architecture.

## Formal designation

**HC-3 Noöplex-EQ — Endocrine-Quantum Synthetic Hyperconnective Neuroglial Encephalon**

## Generation role

HC-3 inherits the complete HC-2 architecture and adds a richer HC-owned neuroendocrine, autonomic, interoceptive, and embodied affective physiology layer.

HC-3 does not create affect, empathy, sexuality, motivation, or social cognition from nothing. Those capacities already have architectural homes in HC-1. HC-3 adds a more physiologically coupled substrate for modulation, body-state participation, endocrine dynamics, autonomic response, and embodied expression.

`HC3 = HC2_COMPLETE + DISTRIBUTED_PHYSIOLOGICAL_AFFECTIVE_SUBSTRATE`

## Distributed organ membership

HC-3 physiology may be physically distributed across cranial, cervical, torso, or other body locations and still be part of the HC when those components satisfy the physical-organ membership contract.

Examples may include:

- HC-owned neuroendocrine synthesis modules;
- interoceptive transduction hardware;
- endocrine transport/control modules;
- autonomic integration controllers;
- neuromodulatory delivery or clearance systems;
- body-state sensing modules dedicated to cognition.

A body-owned pump, vessel, organ, or actuator does not become HC substrate merely because it transports an HC signal. Conversely, an HC-owned endocrine or interoceptive module does not become body-peripheral merely because it sits in the torso.

## Affect architecture

HC-3 treats affect as a distributed dynamical process involving interacting:

- appraisal/network state;
- endocrine state;
- neuromodulatory state;
- interoceptive state;
- autonomic state;
- memory and learned associations;
- current context;
- conation and salience;
- body/embodiment state.

No one-hormone/one-emotion mapping is assumed.

A conceptual relationship may be written as:

`A(t) = F[N(t), E(t), I(t), U(t), M(t), C(t), B(t)]`

This is systems notation, not a claim that affect is presently reducible to a known closed-form function.

## Neuroendocrine control

HC-3 may implement functional analogues of stress, metabolic, reproductive/sex-steroid, circadian, social/osmotic, and autonomic catecholaminergic regulation.

These are control systems with synthesis, release, transport, receptor interaction, metabolism, clearance, feedback, and adaptation. They are not static reservoirs of "emotion chemicals."

## Autonomy and intervention

HC-3 must distinguish autonomous internally generated affective regulation from maintenance/configuration intervention.

Engineering interfaces may set safety envelopes, calibration bounds, therapeutic-style interventions, diagnostic modes, or maintenance controls. They do not automatically gain semantic, identity, consent, or action authority.

An implementation may support autonomous endocrine response in which moment-to-moment modulation is generated by HC-internal appraisal, homeostasis, memory, conation, and interoception.

Any override or maintenance path must remain explicit, scoped, auditable, and subordinate to the relevant authority/consent contracts. The base architecture does not assume an unrestricted external administrator may directly impose affective state.

`MAINTENANCE_ACCESS != AFFECTIVE_AUTHORITY`

`ENDOCRINE_CONTROL != CONSENT_CONTROL`

## Interoception

HC-3 may ingest and model distributed internal-state signals such as:

- temperature;
- pressure/flow;
- oxygenation or redox proxies where relevant;
- pH/osmolarity;
- endocrine concentration;
- damage/nociceptive state;
- actuator or tissue load/fatigue;
- respiratory/cardiac-like rhythms where present;
- thermal and energy reserves.

Raw body telemetry remains distinct from HC-internal interpretation and body-state inference.

## Autonomic integration

HC-3 may coordinate body-facing autonomic effects including pump/heart-like rate, ventilation, vascular tone, pupil state, skin temperature, perspiration analogue, gastrointestinal analogue, muscle readiness, respiratory/vocal support, and endocrine release.

These effects pass through HC-owned action/effect pathways and relevant safety/authority gates. Affective modulation does not bypass kinesis or action authorization.

## Embodied expression

HC-3 can couple affective state to gaze, posture, prosody, facial movement, gesture, tactile behavior, movement dynamics, and other embodied expression.

Expression remains an output of distributed cognition and body control; it is not evidence that an external observer can directly infer private internal state with certainty.

## Learning and calibration

HC-3 may require additional calibration for endocrine response, autonomic regulation, interoceptive interpretation, expressive motor control, and recovery from high-arousal or dysregulated states.

This development may occur before embodiment, after embodiment, in simulation, through supervised calibration, or continuously. No universal fixed pre-installation training sequence is required by the architecture.

## Thermal and resource design

HC-3 adds pumps, sensing, chemical synthesis/clearance, transport, and control overhead to HC-2. Exact budgets are implementation-specific.

Thermal isolation must protect neural/biohybrid substrate from accelerator and chemical-reactor hot/cold domains. Hardware requiring extreme cooling may remain HC-internal when physically distributed and isolated under the physical-organ membership contract.

Detailed support and distributed-organ rules are defined in `docs/engineering/POWER_THERMAL_AND_DISTRIBUTED_ORGAN.md`.

## Failure and graceful degradation

HC-3 should expose endocrine, autonomic, interoceptive, and neuromodulatory faults as typed HC state rather than silently converting them into personality or value change.

Possible degraded modes include:

- reduced endocrine coupling;
- reduced autonomic modulation;
- an HC-2-like capability envelope while remaining a degraded HC-3;
- quarantined faulty physiology module;
- bounded affective flattening or altered modulation caused by subsystem loss.

`DEGRADED_HC3 != HC2`

A fault may change affective state, salience, learning pressure, or behavior without rewriting autobiographical history, identity authority, consent state, or semantic truth.

## Evidence boundary

Endocrine, autonomic, neuromodulatory, interoceptive, and affective interactions are component scientific domains informing HC-3. A fully programmable distributed synthetic system reproducing them at human fidelity is an engineering extrapolation, not a demonstrated complete system.

HC-3 is therefore science-constrained speculative engineering, not a description of current technology or proof of subjective experience. Scientific claims are governed by `docs/science/EVIDENCE_BOUNDARIES.md` and require appropriate source support before receiving `DOCUMENTED` status.
