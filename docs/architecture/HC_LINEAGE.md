# Hyperconnectome HC-Series Lineage

Status: canonical lineage architecture.

## Purpose

The HC-series is a lineage of complete Hyperconnectome cognitive organs. Later generations extend earlier ones without retroactively redefining or erasing them.

The lineage is:

```text
HC-1 Noöplex
  -> HC-2 Noöplex-Q
    -> HC-3 Noöplex-EQ
```

The arrow means architectural extension, not replacement.

`HC_N_PLUS_1_EXTENDS_HC_N`

`EXTENSION != DELETION_OF_PRIOR_GENERATION`

`GENERATION_LABEL != IDENTITY`

Every generation remains a complete cognitive organ under the repository-wide invariants:

- no essential cognition outside the HC;
- one HC cognitive organ may contain physically distributed constituent hardware;
- the HC is a typed, attributed, multilayer temporal hypergraph;
- the architecture is non-hemispheric by default;
- mandatory canonical capacities remain architecturally present even when disabled, dormant, immature, degraded, or unimplemented;
- body/peripheral hardware remains replaceable through HC-owned interfaces.

## HC-1 — Noöplex

HC-1 is the foundational HC-series cognitive organ.

Its defining role is to establish the complete synthetic cognitive architecture without relying on quantum acceleration or HC-3-class distributed endocrine physiology.

HC-1 includes the complete latent cognitive capability set defined by the canonical root systems: cognition, semantics, pragmatics, memory, self-model/identity continuity, affect, empathy, sexuality, social cognition, conation/volition, attention/salience, learning/plasticity, body-schema integration, action selection, and the other mandatory HC systems.

HC-1 must therefore not be treated as psychologically incomplete merely because later generations add richer physical substrates or regulatory machinery.

### Substrate direction

The HC-1 Noöplex lineage is grounded in an engineered neuroglial/biohybrid brain concept rather than a silicon-only conventional computer. Present-day realizability is not assumed.

Human neurobiology may inform mechanisms such as distributed recurrent processing, glial support, plasticity, neuromodulation, memory consolidation, homeostatic regulation, and sensorimotor learning, but human lobes, hemispheres, commissures, or named gross anatomical regions are not mandatory HC topology.

### What HC-1 does not require

HC-1 does not require:

- a quantum processing unit;
- cryogenic hardware;
- HC-3-class synthetic endocrine organ arrays;
- body-distributed affective chemistry;
- human hemispheric packaging.

It may still implement affect, interoception, valuation, empathy, sexuality, motivation, and embodied learning through its HC-1 substrate and interfaces.

## HC-2 — Noöplex-Q

HC-2 extends HC-1 with specialized quantum, photonic, quantum-sensing, or related accelerator capabilities.

The central rule is:

> **Quantum capability augments the HC; it does not become a separate cognitive center.**

HC-2 retains the full HC-1 cognitive organ and adds a bounded accelerator layer for workloads where the selected hardware provides a justified advantage.

Candidate workloads may include selected simulation, sampling, optimization, sensing, chemistry/materials computation, cryptographic, or other specialized workloads. The architecture does not assume generic quantum acceleration for ordinary language, perception, memory, affect, or general cognition.

### Physical membership

An HC-2 QPU may be physically cranial, torso-mounted, or otherwise distributed.

If the QPU uniquely implements an essential HC-2 cognitive capability, it is an HC-owned constituent inside the cognitive-organ boundary even if it occupies a separate enclosure.

If the QPU is optional acceleration and its removal reduces performance without removing essential cognition or continuity, it may be classified as an external computational peripheral.

`LOCATION != ORGAN_MEMBERSHIP`

`QPU_PRESENT != QPU_IS_IDENTITY`

`QPU_RESULT != HC_BELIEF_OR_DECISION`

A cryogenic implementation may therefore use a physically remote or torso-located HC-owned module if the full module is part of the removable HC constituent set. An ordinary external cloud/cold-dock accelerator is not part of the HC unless the architecture explicitly classifies and integrates it as an HC constituent.

### HC-1 tradeoff advantages

HC-1 remains independently valuable. Compared with HC-2, it may retain advantages in simplicity, power, cooling burden, fault surface, calibration burden, maintenance, mass, and deterministic availability on ordinary cognitive tasks.

Later generation does not mean universally superior under every engineering objective.

## HC-3 — Noöplex-EQ

HC-3 extends the complete HC-2 organ with a richer synthetic neuroendocrine, autonomic, neuromodulatory, interoceptive, and outward-expression physiology.

The purpose is not to add the abstract existence of affect, desire, motivation, empathy, sexuality, or social cognition for the first time. Those are already part of the complete latent HC architecture.

HC-3 adds a more deeply embodied physical regulatory substrate through which those capacities can couple to distributed internal physiology, learning, memory, salience, motivation, body state, and expression.

### Systemic affect rule

HC-3 must not encode one-hormone/one-emotion mappings.

Affective state is treated as a coupled dynamical product of, at minimum where implemented:

- cognitive appraisal;
- neuromodulation;
- endocrine state;
- autonomic state;
- interoceptive evidence;
- memory and learned association;
- current context;
- body/embodiment state;
- ongoing regulation and prediction.

Endocrine chemistry is a modulatory/control layer within a larger HC cognitive system, not a magic emotion source.

### Distributed physical membership

HC-3 may physically distribute endocrine synthesis, chemical transport, autonomic-control, interoceptive, neuromodulatory, or related machinery through multiple body locations.

A distributed component belongs to the HC when it is part of the essential HC-3 cognitive-regulatory machinery under the rules in `PHYSICAL_ORGAN_MEMBERSHIP.md`.

Body-support or effect-delivery machinery remains peripheral when the HC retains the cognitive state, interpretation, regulation, learned mappings, and decision machinery internally.

For example, a body pump may be peripheral while the HC-owned regulator that interprets interoceptive state and determines target regulation remains part of the HC. Conversely, a torso-mounted chemical-regulation module whose internal state and dynamics are essential to HC-3 cognition may be an HC constituent rather than body hardware.

## Inheritance and compatibility

Each generation inherits the prior generation's cognitive architecture unless an explicit later decision records a deliberate incompatible change.

A later implementation may refine mechanisms, replace substrates, or add capabilities, but it must preserve the semantic responsibility of inherited essential systems.

The lineage should support graceful degradation where physically possible:

```text
HC-3 generation-specific subsystem loss
  -> HC-2-like reduced capability mode

HC-2 generation-specific accelerator loss
  -> HC-1-like reduced capability mode
```

This is an engineering target, not a guarantee that every fault allows clean downgrade. A failure may instead produce `DEGRADED`, `FAULTED`, or `QUARANTINED` states when inherited capability cannot be preserved safely.

## Embodiment independence across generations

HC-1, HC-2, and HC-3 are cognitive-organ generations, not body models.

Any generation may in principle be installed into a compatible embodiment that provides the required physical support and interfaces. A body change may require recalibration, sensorimotor remapping, interoceptive remapping, body-schema adaptation, safety revalidation, or motor relearning.

Embodiment change does not by itself change HC generation or identity.

## Evidence boundary

The HC-series intentionally extends beyond present-day demonstrated technology.

Accordingly:

- neurobiology and biohybrid engineering may constrain HC-1 plausibility without proving full HC-1 realizability;
- existing quantum and photonic technologies may constrain HC-2 design without proving a compact integrated HC-2;
- known endocrine, autonomic, interoceptive, and affective neuroscience may constrain HC-3 design without proving a programmable synthetic HC-3 physiology;
- architectural completeness does not prove consciousness, personhood, subjective experience, or human-equivalent cognition.

Detailed generation documents should preserve these claim ceilings rather than presenting speculative engineering as demonstrated fact.

## Canonical lineage invariant

> **HC-1 establishes the complete foundational cognitive organ; HC-2 adds specialized quantum/photonic acceleration; HC-3 adds richer distributed embodied neuroendocrine/interoceptive/autonomic affective physiology. Later generations extend rather than psychologically complete earlier ones.**
