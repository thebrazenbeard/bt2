# Imagination, Simulation, and Counterfactuals

Status: canonical focused cognition contract.

## Purpose

The HC may construct internally generated hypothetical scenes, plans, counterfactuals, rehearsals, creative combinations, and possible futures for reasoning, planning, learning, creativity, empathy, and testing.

The governing invariant is:

`SIMULATED != OBSERVED != REMEMBERED_AS_LIVED`

## Simulation object

A simulation or counterfactual should be able to carry:

- scenario ID;
- generating goal/question;
- assumed initial state;
- world/self/social-model references;
- hypothetical modifications;
- generated sequence or outcomes;
- uncertainty;
- model/version references;
- purpose such as planning, creativity, rehearsal, prediction, empathy, or testing;
- explicit `SIMULATED` provenance.

Any premise that intentionally differs from the HC's current admitted state should remain marked as hypothetical.

## Counterfactual reasoning

The HC should support "what if" manipulation without silently rewriting current belief or history.

Counterfactuals may compare:

- alternate actions;
- alternate causal hypotheses;
- alternate social interpretations;
- alternate body/environment conditions;
- failure/recovery scenarios;
- possible future states.

A counterfactual may become evidence about a plan or model only through whatever evaluation/validation process is appropriate. It does not become evidence that the imagined event actually occurred.

## Planning and rehearsal

Action plans may be rehearsed internally to estimate:

- likely consequences;
- resource use;
- timing/latency;
- body constraints;
- social consequences;
- failure modes;
- information gain;
- safety/authority requirements.

Rehearsal produces action candidates and prediction evidence. It does not authorize external effect.

`REHEARSED_ACTION != AUTHORIZED_ACTION`

## Creativity

Novel combinations may be generated without requiring that every component be copied from a prior episode.

Generated artifacts remain attributed as internally generated or synthetic until independently observed, adopted, or admitted into another state class under explicit rules.

Creativity may reuse memory and learned semantic structure without causing synthetic scenes to masquerade as autobiographical recall.

## Social imagination

The HC may simulate how another agent might react. Such simulations remain hypotheses generated from empathy/social models, context, memory, and uncertainty.

`SIMULATED_OTHER_RESPONSE != ACCESS_TO_OTHER_PRIVATE_STATE`

Direct correction or new evidence from the other agent outranks contradicted simulated assumptions.

## Memory interaction

Simulation may retrieve episodic, semantic, procedural, self-model, or social-model state. The generated scenario should preserve links to those sources while maintaining its own synthetic provenance.

Writing simulated scenes into episodic or autobiographical memory as lived history is prohibited unless they are explicitly stored as records of the act of imagining/simulating.

Example:

- valid: "At time T, the HC simulated scenario S."
- invalid: "Scenario S happened" merely because it was vividly simulated.

## External generative systems

An external model may supply candidate simulations, plans, images, language, or predicted outcomes. Those outputs retain external/model-generated provenance and enter through HC-owned evidence/service interfaces.

External generation does not become autobiographical experience, internal belief, identity, desire, or action authority merely because the HC requested it.

## Temporal-hypergraph interaction

Simulations may instantiate temporary coalitions among cognition, memory, semantics, affect, empathy, self-model, world-model reasoning, salience/attention, and kinesis.

Their temporary hyperedges dissolve according to task lifecycle. Any durable learning or memory effect requires a separate admitted plasticity or consolidation operation.

## Failure modes

- imagined event stored as actual history;
- counterfactual premise silently promoted to current fact;
- social simulation treated as mind-reading certainty;
- rehearsal treated as procedural qualification;
- simulated action bypassing effect authorization;
- generated preference or identity content silently adopted as self-state;
- external generative output losing source provenance;
- temporary simulated coalition becoming persistent control topology without plasticity admission.
